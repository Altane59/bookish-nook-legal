Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

The short version. Your library stays on your iPhone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data. The only things that leave your phone are screenshots or titles you ask Claude to read, and book titles looked up for their covers. Bookish Nook never stores them.

1. Who we are

Bookish Nook is operated by Altane, based in the United Kingdom. We are the data controller for any personal data processed through the Bookish Nook app. Contact: altanestudios@proton.me.

3. Your screenshots and titles

Nothing is sent until you agree in the app, and you can turn it off at any time in You → Settings. When it's on:

  • Each screenshot is made smaller on your phone, and hidden metadata (EXIF, such as GPS location, device model and timestamps) is removed before it leaves your device.
  • It's sent over HTTPS through our small reading service to Anthropic's Claude AI, used once to read the book titles, and then discarded. Bookish Nook does not save screenshots or titles, and doesn't write them to its logs.
  • Anthropic processes them to produce the result and may keep API inputs for a limited period for safety and abuse detection, as set out in Anthropic's privacy policy. Anthropic does not use them to train its models. Bookish Nook does not have a zero-retention arrangement with Anthropic.

4. Real covers

To show real book covers, the title and author of each book on your list are looked up in Open Library, a free library run by the non-profit Internet Archive. The look-up goes through our server, so Open Library never sees your device. The covers are then saved on your phone. Nothing else about you or your library is sent. You can turn this off in You → Settings → Find real covers.

6. How we use data

  • To read your screenshots and fill in book details when you ask
  • To find real covers for the books on your list
  • To keep the reading service working and fair for everyone
  • To unlock and restore purchases

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising or track you across other apps and websites.

7. Files you import or export

When you import a Goodreads or StoryGraph file, or save a backup or spreadsheet, the file is read or made on your phone. It only goes where you send it, using the iPhone's share sheet.

8. Storage and retention

  • Library and settings: on your device until you clear them or delete the app.
  • Screenshots and titles: never stored by Bookish Nook (see section 3).
  • Covers: saved on your device with your library. Open Library and Cloudflare may cache the public cover images.
  • Usage counter: expires automatically within 14 days.
  • Purchase records: kept by Apple.

9. Your rights under UK GDPR

You have the right to access, delete, port, correct, restrict and object to the processing of your personal data. To use any of these rights, email altanestudios@proton.me. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Because we hold almost no personal data (no account, no stored screenshots, and a library kept only on your device), most rights are met automatically. You → Your library → Clear my library wipes your library from your device, and Save a backup or Save as spreadsheet gives you a copy of everything.

10. Children

Bookish Nook is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal data from them.

11. Changes

We'll update the date at the top when this policy changes. If a change affects what leaves your phone, the app will tell you and ask again.
