Help and support

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Bookish Nook is a cozy iPhone app that turns screenshots of book recommendations into a to-read list, kept in a little pixel library where a mini you reads along with a cat.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email altanestudios@proton.me. A screenshot of what you're seeing and your iPhone model help a lot. We read every message.

Where are my books saved?

On your iPhone. There's no account, and your library isn't stored on our servers. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, your library is included in that backup.

How do I keep a copy of my library?

Open You → Your library. Save a backup keeps everything (books, notes, mini you), and you can restore it later with Import a file. Save as spreadsheet makes a file Goodreads and StoryGraph can read.

Can I bring my books from Goodreads or StoryGraph?

Yes. Export your library from Goodreads (My Books, then Import and export, then Export library) or StoryGraph (Manage account, then Export StoryGraph library), then choose Import a file in You → Your library. Books already on your list are skipped.

How does screenshot reading work?

When you add a screenshot, it's sent to Claude, an AI made by Anthropic, which finds the book titles in it. You check each book before it's added. Bookish Nook never stores your screenshots. The app asks before it sends anything, and you can turn screenshot reading off in You → Settings and type titles instead. Full details are in the Privacy Policy.

Claude got a book wrong

Tap Not the right one on the card, or open the book later and tap Edit details. AI can make mistakes, which is why every book is shown to you before it's added.

I got a new phone. How do I get my purchase back?

Open You and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with. Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

Open You → Your library → Clear my library, or delete the app. Because your library lives only on your phone, that removes it. See the Privacy Policy for the small amount of data held anywhere else.
